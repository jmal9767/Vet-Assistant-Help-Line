Medical concern or emergency? Contact a veterinarian now.

This app and website are not monitored emergency services. Do not wait for a Veterinary Assistant Help Line response if your pet has symptoms, an injury, suspected poisoning, or anything that worries you.

  • Trouble breathing
  • Seizure, collapse, or severe weakness
  • Heavy or uncontrolled bleeding
  • Major trauma or possible broken bone
  • Bloated abdomen or unproductive retching
  • Unable to urinate
  • Pale, gray, or blue gums
  • Suspected toxin or medication exposure

Call your veterinarian or nearest emergency veterinary hospital before traveling when possible. Use your phone’s Maps app to search for “emergency veterinarian.”

Clear boundaries

What the app is—and is not—for

The service is independently provided by a veterinary assistant, not a licensed veterinarian, and no veterinarian supervises the service. It offers general education only and does not create a veterinarian-client-patient relationship.

Eligible education topics

  • General, nonmedical grooming concepts
  • General enrichment and household-routine basics
  • Crate, litter, and new-pet environment concepts
  • Organizing nonmedical questions for a routine veterinary visit

Always directed to a veterinarian

  • Symptoms, injuries, illness, pain, or urgent concerns
  • Diagnosis, prognosis, or treatment decisions
  • Medication, dose, supplement, food, diet, or product recommendations
  • Behavior or safety assessments and individualized plans
  • Interpretation of laboratory results, imaging, or medical records
  • Questions that could delay or replace veterinary care

Provider-finding, cost, purchase-support, privacy, and emergency questions never require a paid Question Credit.

Native iOS flow

How one Question Credit works

There is no web submission or web checkout. The guided question and purchase flow stays inside the native iOS app; the app then prepares an email that you decide whether to send.

  1. Choose a category

    Select the nonmedical education topic that best matches your question.

  2. Answer one guided prompt

    Write one general question using the prompt for that category. Safety questions route medical and emergency concerns out of the paid flow.

  3. Purchase through Apple

    Buy one Question Credit in the iOS app. Apple shows the current price and may charge when you approve the purchase, before the question email is prepared.

  4. Review and send the email

    After Apple verifies the purchase, review the prepared nonmedical education question and send it using Apple Mail or another email app. Once Apple Mail displays the complete email, a saved, cancelled, failed, or unknown result stays unfinished but is locked until you confirm sent or every copy deleted without sending; a copied or opened fallback is locked the same way. Cancelling fallback before copying leaves the credit reusable. Mail queuing or your sent confirmation finishes the credit; this does not prove inbox delivery or defer Apple's charge. Bay Area Apps verifies fresh Apple Production status and accepts each transaction only once before treating an email as a paid question.
