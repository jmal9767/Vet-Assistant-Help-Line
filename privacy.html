Bay Area Apps LLC

Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

This policy explains how Bay Area Apps LLC ("Bay Area Apps," "we," "us," or "our") handles information for the Veterinary Assistant Help Line website and native iOS app.

Information we handle

Public website

The website has no question form, user account, advertising, analytics, or tracking technology. The site code does not set cookies. Infrastructure used to deliver and secure the site may receive standard connection data, such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, and access time, and handles it under the provider’s terms and retention practices.

Question and support email

The app has no account system or submission server. Its question flow asks you to choose a category and enter one general question; it does not ask for a name or pet details. The app adds a StoreKit-bound submission reference, Apple transaction reference, environment, and Apple-signed transaction proof, then prepares an email addressed to us. The signed proof can contain product, purchase-time, price, currency, storefront, transaction, app-account-token, environment, and related App Store metadata. You choose whether to send that email using Apple Mail or another email provider.

Draft question text stays in the app while you complete the flow and is not intentionally sent to Bay Area Apps before you send the prepared email. Opening a mail composer shares the prepared content with the device’s mail service even if you later cancel; that provider handles it under its own terms. The app records limited purchase and email-handoff state so an unfinished credit is not lost or silently duplicated. After Apple Mail displays the complete email, every result except queued must be resolved as sent or every copy deleted; a copied or opened fallback requires the same resolution. Pending-purchase state remains until resolved. To protect against reuse after an App Store account change or interrupted finish, transaction-ID handoff/use markers may remain until the relevant handoff is resolved or the app’s data is removed. The last used transaction reference remains readable for purchase support for 90 days and its stored keys are removed on the next app launch after expiry. A fallback copy places the full question and Apple-signed proof on the device’s system clipboard, where another app may be able to read it under iOS permissions. It is marked local to this device, set to expire after 10 minutes, and cleared on confirmation or cancellation when it has not already been replaced.

When you send a question or contact support, we receive your sender email address, sender display name if supplied by your mail provider, and the information in that message. Your email provider and ours also process the correspondence. Do not enter names, contact details, pet demographics or history, symptoms, injury, exposure, urgency, behavior or safety concerns, products, foods, diets, supplements, doses, treatments, medications, records, test results, or image information. Do not send street addresses, payment-card details, government identification numbers, human medical information, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Apple StoreKit purchases

Question Credits are purchased inside the iOS app through Apple StoreKit. Apple displays the price and currency and processes your payment. The app validates Apple’s signed transaction result on your device and keeps a verified unfinished transaction available as a Question Credit until Apple Mail reports the email queued or you explicitly confirm sending through the fallback flow. Before accepting a message as a new paid question, Bay Area Apps verifies the emailed proof and current transaction state with Apple’s official server tools. A separate operator ledger atomically stores keyed hashes of the verified transaction and submission references, plus environment and redemption time, so the same purchase cannot be accepted twice; it does not store the sender email, JWS, or question content. Bay Area Apps does not receive your payment-card information. Apple handles purchase information under its own privacy policy.

If verification is temporarily unavailable and an incoming message includes an unexpected attachment that must be deleted, Bay Area Apps may extract only the exact signed JWS into a random-reference, proof-only temporary file on an encrypted operator volume. That file contains no sender address, question, subject, attachment, or other message content; it is owner-only, excluded from backup and synchronization, retained only while the payment hold is unresolved, and deleted immediately after a definitive verification result.

The native app contains no advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

How we use information

  • Process or troubleshoot a Question Credit and provide the app feature you requested; StoreKit verification occurs on the customer’s device.
  • Review each valid paid or authorized replacement message and provide an eligible educational answer or a scope/referral notice.
  • Answer app, purchase, privacy, and technical support requests.
  • Prevent misuse, protect the service, and comply with legal obligations.

Responses are independently provided by a veterinary assistant who is not a licensed veterinarian and is not supervised by a veterinarian through this service. We do not use question or support content to diagnose, treat, prescribe for, or establish a veterinarian-client-patient relationship.

How information is disclosed

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising. We disclose information only as needed to operate the service, including:

  • Apple, for StoreKit purchase processing, validation, fraud prevention, and App Store support.
  • Email and hosting providers that deliver messages or website content on our behalf.
  • Authorities or other parties when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate fraud or abuse, or respond to a valid legal request.

Bay Area Apps requires providers it selects to protect information consistently with this policy and use it only for the contracted service. Bay Area Apps configures retention settings it controls to follow this policy; provider backups, quarantine, and security records may remain for limited contractual schedules. A mail provider you independently choose is governed by your agreement with that provider.

Retention

We delete or de-identify operator-controlled question, answer, and ordinary support correspondence 90 days after the final response or closure, whichever applies. A thread with an offered replacement is kept through its stated deadline; if no timely replacement arrives, that deadline is its closure date and the thread is deleted or de-identified 90 days later. If a timely replacement arrives, its correspondence is kept until 90 days after its final response or closure. Replay-prevention ledger hashes are kept for the life of the paid service and deleted within 90 days after it truly ends, unless a documented legal, security, dispute, fraud, accounting, or transaction need requires a defined longer period. The service is not treated as ended until sales stop and device credits not marked sent, signed proofs awaiting reconciliation, payment holds, paid messages already handed off, open questions, disputes, and replacement windows are honored or otherwise resolved. We configure operator-controlled provider retention to follow this policy; limited provider backups, quarantine, and security records may follow contractual schedules.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. No website, device, or email system can be guaranteed completely secure. Email may be stored by the providers and devices involved, so send only the information needed for your request.

Your choices and requests

You may ask to access, correct, or delete correspondence we control by emailing info@bayareaapps.com with the subject “Privacy Request.” We may need to verify your request and may retain information when permitted or required by law. Minimal keyed redemption hashes may remain for replay prevention because deleting them would allow the same purchase proof to be reused. StoreKit billing, purchase-history, and refund requests may also need to be handled through Apple.

Children

The paid service is only for adults age 18 or older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

External services and policy changes

Links to Apple, emergency-veterinary resources, and other external services are governed by those services’ own policies. We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
