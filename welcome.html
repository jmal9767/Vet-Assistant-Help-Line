🐾 Vet Assistant Help Line

Welcome! Here's how it works.

What this is

Worried about your pet and not sure what to do next? This help line is run by a veterinary assistant with hands-on experience in both emergency and general practice. Send the details, choose email, text, or phone, and attach helpful photos, videos, records, or labels for practical guidance.

  • Identifying general warning signs and helping you choose an appropriate next step
  • General pet care, feeding, and husbandry questions
  • Grooming, enrichment, and basic behavior tips
  • Home care & recovery questions after a vet visit
  • Photos, videos, PDFs, records, labels, and other files for context
  • Preparing for vet or emergency visits & what to ask
  • New-pet, senior-pet, and routine-care basics (vaccine schedules, parasite prevention)

What I can't do: I'm a veterinary assistant, not a licensed veterinarian — no diagnoses, no medication or dosing advice, and no substitute for a vet exam.

How it works

  1. Tap Ask your question, choose email, text, or phone, and add any helpful files.
  2. I choose complimentary service or confirm a price before sending a payment link.
  3. Your answer arrives in the chosen channel. Response timing is confirmed after review. If a paid response does not cover the agreed question, you may request a refund review.
