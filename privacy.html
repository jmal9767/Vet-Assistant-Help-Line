Privacy policy

Updated September 6, 2026. Contact: info@bayareaapps.com.

Information used to provide the service

The service stores your reply email, selected category, general question, optional nonmedical context, requested answer format, the terms you accepted, relevant dates, answer, clarification, payment references and status, refund information, and reply status. Your draft is saved when you prepare checkout. Please do not submit symptoms, medical histories, records, photos, names of other people, addresses, passwords, or card details.

Who receives it

Jahmal Parris uses this information to respond and manage the service. The hosting provider stores the service database. Stripe processes payment and receives your checkout email, amount, general service description, question reference, and checkout return links. Stripe collects payment information directly; this app does not store card numbers. The nonmedical question and answer text are not sent to Stripe.

Apple Mail and the configured email providers handle the answer email, your reply address, and related support correspondence. Their systems may retain messages independently. No AI provider receives your question. There are no advertising trackers or analytics in this application, and information is not sold or used for advertising.

Your private question link

Anyone with your complete private link can read the question and use its available actions. Save it securely and do not forward it. The checkout return link is supplied to Stripe and may remain in your browser history.

The intake page temporarily saves the question reference and private link information in your browser tab to help recover interrupted checkout. It does not save question text in that tab storage. Your question and answer remain accessible through your private link.

Retention and requests

Completed question records are normally removed from the service database 90 days after the most recent recorded activity, once outstanding replies and service obligations are resolved. Unpaid checkout drafts are eligible for removal after seven days once checkout is confirmed to have expired without payment. Records are kept longer while payment, an answer, or a refund still needs to be resolved.

Short-lived security records help prevent misuse. Hosting providers may also process network information. Deleting a service record does not itself delete email, payment records, backups, or records required for accounting or legal obligations; those systems have separate retention. Contact support to request access, correction, deletion, or help with a lost private link. Ownership must be verified, and required transaction records may need to be retained.

Security

The service uses HTTPS and restricted access to protect submitted information. No system can guarantee perfect security. Do not send sensitive or unnecessary information; contact support if your private link is lost or shared accidentally.

See the Terms for service scope, payment, response timing, and refunds.
