What this is

Not sure whether your pet needs a vet — or the emergency room? This help line is run by a veterinary assistant with hands-on experience in both emergency and general practice, so you get a thorough, honest answer that helps you make the right call for your pet — plus help with everyday questions like feeding, grooming, and behavior. Replies come by email within 24–48 hours.

  • Helping you decide whether something is an emergency — or can wait for your regular vet
  • General pet care, feeding, and husbandry questions
  • Grooming, enrichment, and basic behavior tips
  • Home care & recovery questions after a vet visit
  • Preparing for vet or emergency visits & what to ask
  • New-pet, senior-pet, and routine-care basics (vaccine schedules, parasite prevention)

What I can't do: I'm a veterinary assistant, not a licensed veterinarian — no diagnoses, no medication or dosing advice, and no substitute for a vet exam.

How it works

  1. Tap Ask your question below and send in your question — add your phone number if you'd prefer your reply by text message.
  2. I confirm the price with you by email and send a secure payment link — you never pay before agreeing.
  3. Your answer arrives in your chosen time frame — standard is email within 24–48 hours, with same-day, express, text, and phone options available. Satisfaction guarantee: if you don't feel your question was answered, you get a full refund.
