Privacy policy

Updated September 6, 2026. Contact: info@bayareaapps.com.

Information used to provide the service

The service stores your reply email, selected category, general question, optional nonmedical context, requested answer format, accepted catalog version, timestamps, answer, clarification, payment references and status, refund information, and reply handoff status. Your draft is stored when you prepare checkout; payment is verified before it reaches the operator’s paid inbox. Please do not submit symptoms, medical histories, records, photos, names of other people, addresses, passwords, or card details.

Who receives it

Jahmal Parris uses this information to respond and manage the service. The hosting provider stores the service database. Stripe processes payment and receives your checkout email, amount, general service description, question reference, and checkout return links. Stripe collects payment information directly; this app does not store card numbers. The nonmedical question and answer text are not sent to Stripe.

Apple Mail and the configured email providers handle the answer email, your reply address, and related support correspondence. Their systems may retain messages independently. No AI provider receives your question. There are no advertising trackers or analytics in this application, and information is not sold or used for advertising.

Private links and your device

Your private question link contains a secret access key. Anyone with the complete link can read the question and use its available actions. Save it securely and do not forward it. The key is sent to this service in a request header; only its hash is stored in the service database. The full checkout return link is also supplied to Stripe and may remain in your browser history. This is an access link, not an account password.

The intake page uses temporary, per-tab browser storage for the question reference and access key to recover an interrupted checkout. It does not store question text there. The private operator app keeps client questions in memory while unlocked and stores its access credential in that iPhone’s Keychain. Backgrounding locks the inbox. Sent or saved email drafts remain in Mail until managed there.

Retention and requests

Completed question records are normally removed from the service database 90 days after the most recent recorded activity, provided there is no unresolved reply handoff or service obligation. Unpaid checkout drafts are eligible for removal after seven days once Stripe confirms that checkout expired without payment. Uncertain payment states, unconfirmed checkout creation, outstanding answers, and unresolved refunds are retained for reconciliation instead of risking loss of a paid question.

Rate-limit records use a keyed hash of the network address and are short-lived; raw network addresses may be handled by hosting infrastructure. Database deletion does not itself delete email, payment records, server backups, or records required for accounting or legal obligations. Those systems have separate retention. Contact support to request access, correction, deletion, or help with a lost private link. Ownership must be verified; required transaction records may need to be retained.

Security

Service traffic uses HTTPS. The private inbox requires its device access key and the iPhone’s passcode or biometric unlock. The database is kept outside public files with restricted filesystem permissions. No system can guarantee perfect security. Do not send sensitive or unnecessary information; contact support if a private link or device is lost.
